              Models used: none

              Every step is made by ordinary code inside this page: reading the files, grouping by rule, and working out rhythm, price changes and totals. No model, local or remote, reads your statements, and the page is not allowed to send anything anywhere, not even to the small local server that opened this window.

              How the list is worked out, and what is kept

              Only money paid out is listed. A line is a bank name with 3 or more payments in 3 or more calendar months. The bank’s description is cut at the first “ | ” (card rows are merchant, then location), words with digits are dropped unless nothing else is left, web endings, dots and a trailing UK, LTD or PLC are dropped, single letters are joined, and after a “*” the name is kept when the part before it is a short processor name. No list of shops is used, so payment platforms that pay many sellers stay as one line.

              A year is the total for the period you added, scaled to 365 days. A price change is shown when runs of the same amount, at least two payments long, cover 80% or more of a line’s payments, with at most three changes. Last paid is shown up front when the time since the last payment is more than twice the usual gap, and at least 45 days.

              New in the latest statement lists every name with no payment before the first payment in your latest statement, largest first. “First time” only means not in the earlier statements you added. Every one is listed, however small. It is a list of what is new, with no view on whether a payment should be there.

              The running balance is checked in each file. A break is shown, never fixed. Money coming in is not listed.

              Nothing is stored. Your statements, renames, merges and the £ limit are held in this window’s memory only, and are gone when you close or reload it. Save the list as CSV to keep it. The bank’s own names always stay visible.
