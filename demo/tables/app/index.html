About this small experiment

For images, this cup uses PaddleOCR’s PP-OCRv6 text recogniser and SLANet-plus table model, packaged by RapidAI. They run on your computer. Your file is not sent to a cloud service.

Start with a clear, tightly cropped image of one printed table in English, with no merged cells. Use the rotation and crop controls to make the text upright and remove surrounding page text. Handwriting, tilted photographs and complex layouts may not work well. It can miss spaces, misread numbers or add empty rows.

For a PDF statement, no model is used. Tables reads the words and their positions from the PDF with pdfplumber, finds the columns of amounts, copies a blank date from the row above, and uses the printed running balance to tell money in from money out. Rows where the balance does not add up are marked. Scanned PDFs are not read.

There is no result history. You choose what to save.
