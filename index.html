Kettle Cups

Small tools for everyday tasks.

Kettle is a collection of small tools powered by local AI models. Each tool is a Cup: it does one job on your own computer, gives you a result you can check, and lets you save it.

Start with Tables: turn a photo of a printed table into editable cells, check the result and save a CSV for your spreadsheet.

The first Cup: Tables

Tables takes care of typing out a printed table. Choose a photograph and the local reader fills in a draft grid. Check it against the image, fix any mistakes and save the result.

You can edit every cell and add or remove rows and columns. Future Cups will grow from other everyday jobs where a small local tool can help.

Small models, running locally

Useful AI should fit the job—and the computer you already have. We choose small, specialist models for focused tasks, with results you can check and correct.

Tables uses small specialist AI models to find the table, read its text and arrange the cells. They run on your computer’s CPU, with no account or cloud service needed to read your image.

Setup downloads the models once. After that, you can read tables offline. The four model files total about 34 MB. See which models Tables uses.

About Kettle Cups

Kettle Cups grew out of Kettle, an earlier experiment in reading paperwork with local models. It keeps the focus on small tasks with results you can inspect and correct. Tables is the first Cup.
