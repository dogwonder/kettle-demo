Tools

One job each, with a result you can check

Each tool handles one repeatable job and shows you the result to fix and keep. Your own files stay on your Mac or phone; only public pages go to the cloud. Right now, Funding watch is live and the Mac and iPhone tools aren't available to download yet.

In the cloud

Funding watch

Live

A weekly email telling a small charity what changed on its funders' public pages, with every date and amount pulled from the funder's own words. Live since October 2026 for its first charity.

See how the funding watch works.

On an iPhone

Label, Text and Scan

In development

One app that reads what's in front of you — a food label, a letter or a screenshot — and checks it on the phone. Works without Apple Intelligence and has no networking code. Not out for download yet.

See the iPhone tools.

On a Mac

All six run in the development Kettle app on macOS and aren't available to download yet. Where a tool uses models, setup downloads and verifies them once; after that, nothing uses the network and your files never leave your computer.

Tables

In development

Turn a photo of a printed table, or a PDF bank statement, into cells you can check, edit and save as CSV.

Works on
One clear, upright, tightly cropped image of a printed English table with no merged cells, or a text-PDF bank statement (not a scan).
Models
Four small specialist models (~34 MB total) on CPU for images; none for PDF statements. See the models used.
Check
Every cell before saving. It can misread numbers, drop spaces or add empty rows.

See what Tables does, or read what's been checked. Or try it in your browser.

Meeting Notes

In development

Transcribe a recorded meeting, listen back beside the draft, fix mistakes and save as text.

Works on
An English meeting between two people, up to 30 minutes.
Models
Whisper small.en (~486 MB) on CPU. See the model used.
Check
The transcript against the audio. It can miss or invent words, especially names and numbers.

See what Meeting Notes does.

Text

In development

Sort, tidy and fix line breaks in plain text, and see a count of what changed.

Works on
Pasted or opened plain text: five exact clean-up jobs, one at a time, or comparing two lists.
Models
None. Plain code only, no downloads.
Check
The words sentence case flags, such as names it may have lowercased.

See what Text does. Or try it in your browser.

Share Copy

In development

Strip personal details from a letter or email before you paste it into a chat model.

Works on
Pasted or opened English text, or an image of a printed letter.
Models
Ettin 68M PII suggests details to strip and Tesseract reads images (~141 MB total) on CPU. See the models used.
Check
The whole copy. The suggestions miss things, so the copy is only as safe as your read-through.

See what Share Copy does.

Regulars

In development

Pull every repeat payee out of a year of bank statements, sorted by yearly cost.

Works on
Statement CSVs checked and saved in Tables. One bank's layout so far.
Models
None. Plain code only, nothing stored.
Check
Your statements in Tables first, and save the CSV before closing if you want to keep it.

See what Regulars does. Or try it in your browser.

Media

In development

Shrink a video or picture, crop a photo, convert to black and white, or trim a recording.

Works on
One short landscape MP4, one WAV or MP3 recording, or up to 100 JPEG or PNG pictures.
Models
None. For a few days a 2.5 GB language model read typed requests; Kettle took it out when a better form did the same job.
Check
The written plan before confirming, then look at or listen to the copy.

See what Media does.

Using them together

Some jobs take two tools in a row. You pour the output from one to the next by hand: save the checked file, then open it in the next tool. The playbooks log the routes tested end to end, like taking a year of statements through Tables and into Regulars.

What each tool page covers

  • What goes in, what comes out and what you need to check.
  • Every model it uses, its job and its exact version, or confirmation that it uses none.
  • When it touches the network (setup only on Mac and iPhone).
  • Its real status and what it gets wrong.
